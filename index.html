What Is Vantage Markets?

Vantage Markets provides access to leveraged derivative products, including contracts for difference (CFDs). CFDs allow users to speculate on price movements of an underlying asset without owning the underlying asset. The company offers trading tools, account options, market information and learning resources for different levels of experience.

Demo Trading

Vantage Markets Demo accounts use virtual funds to provide a simulated environment for exploring platform features and practising strategies. A demo environment does not fully reproduce live-market execution, emotions, liquidity or financial risk, so results in a demo account may differ from live trading.

Copy Trading

Copy-trading features allow users to follow or replicate strategies from selected signal providers, where the service is available. Copy trading still involves market risk, and another trader’s historical performance does not guarantee future results. Users should understand the settings and potential losses before using such features.
